# PRODUCT BRIEF — Persona-aware Competitive Intelligence Desk

**What:** a continuous competitive-intelligence service for enterprise strategy teams and VC firms. Natural-language brief in; verified, cited intelligence brief, monitored landscape and persona-specific priorities out.

**Theme:** Research and Markets (primary); Investing (second customer segment).

**Who pays today:** enterprises pay CI analysts, research firms and consultants; VC funds pay associates and data subscriptions for market maps and competitor/portfolio tracking.

**What it does end to end:** resolve companies/people to canonical Crustdata identities → gather current evidence (Enrich, Job, Person, Web) → connect and deduplicate signals into events with atomic, cited claims → score and explain per persona → set up Crustdata Watcher monitoring → learn persona preferences from natural-language feedback through governed proposals.

**Where a person is still needed:** resolving ambiguous entities, approving policy proposals, approving any spend-increasing Watcher change, and making the actual strategy/investment decision.

**Differentiator:** one evidence base, many decision lenses; NL feedback that is typed, validated, simulated, approved, versioned, persona-scoped and reversible.

**Not claimed:** forecasting outcomes, proving causality, real-time monitoring, replacing human judgment.

**Industry-agnostic:** home organization, market, competitors and personas are inputs. FMCG/Godrej config from the legacy product survives only as a labelled legacy example.

Legacy feature status: provenance/claims/fingerprints/scoring/impact — RETAIN/SIMPLIFY; scanners — REVAMP to Crustdata; NL command — REMOVE/REBUILD; single scoring profile — REVAMP; admin panel, delivery channels, Azure, ops copilot — DEFER/REMOVE.
