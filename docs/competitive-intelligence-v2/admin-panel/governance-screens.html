    Feedback & Governance Screens¶

    These screens are the control room for how the system learns. They cover the Feedback & Tuning group (scoring weights, agent configs, legacy suppressions) and the Governance group (natural-language commands, the proposal review queue, mined patterns, the rule library, the weights editor, policy packs, policy health, and live runtime state). Together they let an operator inspect what feedback has produced, approve or reject proposed policy changes, compile and activate policy packs, and see exactly which rules are gating live runs.

    The governance pipeline is deterministic

    Feature extraction, pattern mining, and proposal generation use no LLM — proposal text comes from templates. The only LLM-backed screens here are NL-Command, and the consolidation feature inside Policy Health. See Feedback & Learning Governance.

    Feedback & Tuning¶

    Scoring¶

    Screen: scoring (Feedback & Tuning) · loader loadScoringWeights

    Lists scoring-weight profiles and lets you create, activate, and patch them. Backing endpoints: GET /admin/scoring-weights, POST /admin/scoring-weights (creates, no auto-activate), PUT /admin/scoring-weights/{id}/activate, PATCH /admin/scoring-weights/{id} (merges weight_updates), and GET /admin/scoring-weights/defaults. Exactly one profile is active at a time. See Tune Scoring Weights.

    Agents¶

    Screen: agents (Feedback & Tuning) · loader loadAgentConfigs

    Manages per-agent prompt addenda and feedback. It lists agent configs (GET /admin/agent-configs, GET /admin/agent-configs/{agent_name}), lets you append feedback (POST /admin/agent-configs/{agent_name}/feedback, with feedback_type one of instruction | correction | example | constraint), and reset an agent (DELETE /admin/agent-configs/{agent_name}/reset).

    Suppressions¶

    Screen: suppressions (Feedback & Tuning) · loader loadSuppressions

    Manages the legacy FeedbackSuppressionEntry table (Layer 1 suppression), via GET/POST/DELETE /admin/feedback/suppressions.

    Two suppression systems, not unified

    This screen manages legacy FeedbackSuppressionEntry rows only. Governance SuppressionRule rows (Layer 2) are created by approving proposals and appear under the Rule Library and Runtime State. The two tables are separate. See Manage Suppressions.

    Governance¶

    NL-Command¶

    Screen: nl-command (Governance) · loader (LLM parser)

    A free-text command box that parses a natural-language instruction into a structured governance action and executes it. It posts to POST /admin/feedback/nl-command (LLM). Parsed actions include add_rule, suppress, adjust_weight, compile_pack, deactivate_rule, and query.

    Governance (proposals)¶

    Screen: governance (Governance) · loader loadProposals

    The human-review queue — the heart of governance. It lists pending FeedbackChangeProposal records (with a red badge for the pending count) and lets you approve or reject each one:

    • GET /admin/feedback/proposals?status&limit
    • POST /admin/feedback/proposals/{id}/approve (optionally with edited_change_json)
    • POST /admin/feedback/proposals/{id}/reject

    Approval always activates the proposal regardless of its risk_level. What activation does depends on the proposal type: a suppression_rule becomes an active SuppressionRule; a scoring_weight_change deactivates the current profile and inserts a new active ScoringWeightProfile; an agent_policy_rule creates an active AgentPolicyRule and auto-recompiles that agent's default pack. See Review & Approve Feedback Proposals.

    Patterns¶

    Screen: patterns (Governance) · loader loadPatterns

    Read-only view of mined FeedbackPattern records (GET /admin/feedback/patterns). Each pattern is keyed by error type / agent / failure-mode / category / source-type, with a computed frequency and severity. Patterns are what drive proposal generation once they cross frequency thresholds.

    Rule Library¶

    Screen: rule-library (Governance) · loader loadRuleLibrary

    CRUD over AgentPolicyRule records, backed by GET /admin/feedback/agent-policy-rules, GET /admin/feedback/agent-policy-rules/all?agent_name&status, POST, and PATCH /{id}. This is where the raw policy rules live before they are compiled into packs.

    Weights Editor¶

    Screen: weights-editor (Governance) · loader loadWeightsEditor

    Governance-side view of scoring weight profiles via GET /admin/feedback/weight-profiles/active and POST /admin/feedback/weight-profiles. Complements the Feedback & Tuning Scoring screen with the governance profile lineage.

    Policy Packs¶

    Screen: policy-packs (Governance) · loader loadPolicyPacks (+ loadPolicyRules, loadEvalCases)

    The compile/activate/edit workbench, plus evaluation. Backing endpoints:

    • GET /admin/feedback/policy-packs?agent_name — list packs
    • POST /admin/feedback/policy-packs/compile — compile (3 modes: custom_text, rule_ids, all-active)
    • PATCH /admin/feedback/policy-packs/{id} and POST /admin/feedback/policy-packs/{id}/activate
    • Eval cases: POST /admin/feedback/eval-cases (201), GET ?agent_name, POST /admin/feedback/eval-cases/{id}/approve, and POST /admin/feedback/eval-runs

    Pack budgets

    A pack must fit within 400 tokens and 8 rules and contain no duplicates, or activate is blocked. Per-run budget across packs is 800 tokens. See Compile & Activate Policy Packs and Run the Evaluation Harness.

    Policy Health¶

    Screen: policy-health (Governance) · loader loadPolicyHealth

    The budget and coverage cockpit, backed by /admin/policy-health/*: GET /admin/policy-health?agent_name (pack + latest snapshot), GET /admin/policy-health/{id} (recomputes and persists a snapshot), POST /admin/policy-health/{id}/consolidate (LLM merge/retire/convert → PolicyConsolidationProposal), GET/PATCH on consolidation proposals, GET /admin/policy-health/{id}/coverage-map, and POST /admin/policy-health/simulate (combined-budget simulation). Use it to spot near-budget or over-budget packs, duplicate rules, and coverage gaps.

    Runtime State¶

    Screen: runtime-state (Governance) · loader loadRuntimeState

    The live view of everything currently gating runs — active rules, packs, weight profiles, and suppressions — via GET /admin/feedback/runtime-state. Check this after approving proposals to confirm the change is actually in force.
